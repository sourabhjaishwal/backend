Day 6: Authentication System

4 Pillars of Authtication system

- Authentication: Identify karna ki request kis user ke paas se aayi hai
- Authorization: user kya kya kar sakta hai
- Validation: data ka format check karna - data sahi form me hai ya nahi
- Verification: data sahi hai ya nahi
